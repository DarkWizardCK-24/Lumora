# Lumora in Simple Words

## What is Lumora?
A personal **study tracker** for Windows. You tell it what you want to learn and by when. Then you log the time you study. It shows whether you're on track.

Everything stays **on your computer**. No internet needed.

## The 5 ideas you need

| Idea | Think of it as | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Subject** | A shelf | Python, DSA, SQL |
| **Topic** | A book on the shelf | "Loops", "Joins" |
| **Goal** | A target with a deadline | "Phase 1 — Python foundations, by 30 Nov" |
| **Milestone** | A checkpoint inside a goal | "Score 80% on the phase test" |
| **Session** | One study sitting (a timer) | "45 minutes on loops" |

Flow: **Goal** (what and when) → **Topics** (what to study) → **Sessions** (the time you actually put in) → **Dashboard** (am I on track?)

## Your account and your data
- You sign up with an email and password. Nothing goes online.
- Your data is **locked (encrypted)** and only opens when you sign in.
- At sign up you get a **recovery key**. Save it. It's the only way back in if you forget your password.
- 5 wrong passwords = a short lock (30 seconds, then longer).
- Each account has its own separate data.

## Your Python journey, already inside
When an account opens for the first time, your roadmap is added automatically.

**1. The big goal** — *Python Mastery & Interview Readiness, 30 Sep 2027*
Its checkpoints are your 6 projects:
1. Personal Finance CLI (Nov 2026)
2. Inventory & Billing (Dec 2026)
3. DSA Visualizer (Feb 2027)
4. Financial Data Analyzer (Apr 2027)
5. Finance REST API (Jul 2027)
6. Capstone (Sep 2027)

**2. Eight phase goals** — the roadmap, one step at a time

| Phase | When | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oct–Nov 2026 | Python basics |
| 2 | Dec 2026 | Functions, files, OOP |
| 3 | Jan 2027 | Core DSA |
| 4 | Feb–Mar 2027 | Advanced DSA |
| 5 | Apr 2027 | SQL and data |
| 6 | May–Jun 2027 | Advanced Python, FastAPI |
| 7 | Jul 2027 | Git, Linux, Docker |

Each phase has a checkpoint ("write programs without tutorials") and an **80% assessment**.

**3. One DSA goal** — solve 250–350 problems, with checkpoints at 30–50, 110–150, 180–240 and 250–340.

**4. 42 topics** — your syllabus checklist, filed under Python, DSA, SQL, FastAPI and DevOps. Each is linked to the phase where you learn it.

**5. 3 notes** — your daily routine, the pass rules, and the AI-independence rules plus the mentor prompt.

## What to do each day (the simple loop)
1. Open **Dashboard** and see today's plan.
2. Start a **Session** (timer) and study.
3. Stop the timer when you finish.
4. Tick off the checkpoint or topic when you can do it without help.
5. On Sunday, check **Insights / Analytics** for the week.

## Where to find things

| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| See today and my progress | Dashboard |
| See goals and checkpoints | My Learning |
| See the topic checklist | Subjects |
| Start the timer | Sessions or Focus |
| Log a coding problem | Coding |
| Read my routine and rules | Notes |
| See whether I'll make the deadline | Smart Analysis |
| Plan study blocks | Calendar (generate a plan from a goal) |
| Sign out, change password, picture | Settings → Local account |

## Two honest notes
- The roadmap gives the **plan**. Lumora tracks your time, but it can't tell if you really learned something. Use the 80% tests for that.
- Lost password **and** recovery key means that account's data can't be recovered.
